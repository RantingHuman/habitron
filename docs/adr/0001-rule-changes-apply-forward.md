# Changes to a habit's rules apply forward, not to past days

A habit keeps every version of its rules (its schedule and, for a measurable habit, its target), each with the day it took effect, and judges a past day by the rules in force on that day. We chose this over applying the current rules to all of history (simpler, and how the app first worked) because editing a schedule or target would otherwise silently rewrite past due days and outcomes, and with them old streaks, missed days and the strength score.
